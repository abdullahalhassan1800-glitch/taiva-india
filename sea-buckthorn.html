What's Inside

One fruit, described plainly

Sea Buckthorn Berry

Hippophae rhamnoides — the Himalayan berry itself, pressed for juice. It is a small, thorn-laden fruit that survives temperatures well below freezing.

Natural Polyphenols

Quercetin, kaempferol and isorhamnetin are naturally present in sea buckthorn and are the compounds most often discussed in the research.

Vitamin C-Rich Fruit

Fresh sea buckthorn is one of the more vitamin C-dense fruits per 100 g. How much survives into juice depends on variety and processing, so the exact figure is printed on your pack rather than claimed here.

Honest Labelling

Sea buckthorn is intensely tart, so most juices are sweetened. Our pack states the added-sugar content and the FSSAI licence number exactly as filed — including the word “Sweetened” where it applies.

What The Research Says

Including the parts that do not help us. Sea buckthorn is still a young ingredient in human research, and we would rather tell you the full picture than quote the one trial that flatters the product.

The one positive gut trial

In 2026, 90 adults took a standardised sea buckthorn extract for 28 days. Bowel frequency and stool consistency both improved, with no adverse events reported. Note the word extract — that is not the same as juice, and we are not going to pretend otherwise.

Morone et al., Nutrients 2026;18(5):806 · NCT07082673

The larger juice trials were flat

Two bigger studies of sea buckthorn juice found no meaningful effect on cholesterol or blood lipids, and a 233-participant study found no reduction in colds or infections. So if you buy this for cholesterol, the honest answer is: no good evidence for that.

Larmo et al., 2005 (n=233, 90 days) · Ecological, 229-participant lipid trial

Mostly traditional use

Sea buckthorn has a long record of use in Himalayan, Tibetan and Mongolian wellness practice, including as a food rather than a remedy. Much of the modern excitement is built on laboratory and animal work, which is a low bar.

Directions & Storage

  • Take as directed on the pack label. The serving size and daily intake are printed on the bottle, exactly as filed.
  • Best taken with or after a meal.
  • Shake well before use if pulp is present.
  • Refrigerate after opening and consume within the period stated on the pack.

Important Safety Information

  • Blood clotting: sea buckthorn may reduce platelet aggregation. Speak to your doctor before use if you take warfarin, aspirin or clopidogrel, and before any scheduled surgery.
  • Blood sugar: it may lower blood glucose. Speak to your doctor if you take diabetes medication.
  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding: not recommended without medical advice.
  • This is a food beverage, not a medicine. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.

If you have a medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, or take regular medication, please consult a qualified healthcare professional before use.
